Privacy Policy

MenuWidgets · last updated October 8, 2026

MenuWidgets is a Mac app that runs entirely on your own computer. There is no MenuWidgets server, and the app sends your data to no one.

What it reads from Google

When you sign in with Google, the Mail widget asks for the gmail.metadata scope: read-only access to message labels and headers. With it, the app lists the unread messages in your inbox and reads each one's sender, subject, date and the short snippet Gmail provides. It cannot read message bodies or attachments, and it cannot send, change or delete anything.

What it does with it

That information is shown in the app's menu-bar panel and in macOS notifications on your Mac, and is kept in memory only while the app runs. It is not written to disk, logged, sold, shared, used for advertising or sent anywhere other than back to Google when the app asks for updates.

What it stores

The sign-in token Google issues is saved in your Mac's login Keychain. Nothing else from your Google account is stored.

Removing access

Sign out from the Mail panel, or remove MenuWidgets at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Either ends the app's access.

Google API Services User Data Policy

MenuWidgets' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
