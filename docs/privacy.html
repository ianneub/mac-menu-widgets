Privacy Policy

MenuWidgets · last updated October 16, 2026

MenuWidgets is a Mac app that runs entirely on your own computer. There is no MenuWidgets server, and the app sends your data to no one.

Gmail

MenuWidgets doesn't connect to Google. The Mail widget gets your Gmail through PIM Bridge, a separate app on your Mac that holds the Google sign-in, over a local socket. From the bridge it gets the unread messages in your inbox, with each one's sender, subject, date, a short snippet and a link to open it in Gmail. When you click a conversation's trash can, it asks the bridge to move that conversation to Gmail's Trash, or back out of it if you click Undo. It doesn't read message bodies or attachments, and it can't send anything. PIM Bridge's own documentation covers what it asks Google for.

What it does with it

That information is shown in the app's menu-bar panel and in macOS notifications on your Mac, and is kept in memory only while the app runs. It is not written to disk, logged, sold, shared, used for advertising or sent anywhere.

What it stores

Nothing from your Google account. Sign-ins live in PIM Bridge.

Removing access

Set mail.gmail to false in MenuWidgets' settings, or sign the account out from the Mail panel (which signs it out of PIM Bridge for every app that uses it).
